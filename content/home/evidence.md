This is **annual token spend**, not license savings, 83% fewer tokens, an audited
cost-per-result comparison, or 83% lower full TCO. “Lower LLM costs” is the short

quality across the before and after periods.

## Method and comparison discipline

The owner-provided Gartner materials inform the editorial method, not the Tur.ai
numbers: **G00798168**, pp. 6–9 (a clear comparison point, quantified outcomes and
cross-industry storylines); **G00762343**, pp. 4–6, 8–10 (outcome/situation/impact/
resolution, authentic case proof and progressive engagement); **G00805945**,
pp. 4, 10 (a demo proves a relevant message rather than showing everything);
**G00821433**, pp. 4–6 (use-case-specific buyer orientation).
No licensed report text is republished and no Gartner endorsement is implied.

Competitor case studies, commissioned composite studies, guarantees, price lists
and matched benchmarks are different evidence types. No unverified comparison
from an earlier chat is promoted into this rewrite. A competitive TCO or token
efficiency test needs a named baseline, the same workload and acceptance criteria,
the cost boundary, period, sample, and a before/after method.
