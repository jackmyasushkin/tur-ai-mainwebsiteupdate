# Home business brief — operational plane

Recorded 13 October 2026. **Direction approved; full replacement copy proposed for review.**
This brief supersedes the previous “awaiting positioning” placeholder.

## Owner direction

The owner asked for the main Tur.ai site to express the common denominator:
**“operational plane for regulated processes above your systems of record”.**
Banking and other vertical pages will follow; Home is not a banking-only landing page.

The owner confirmed the proposed shift from customer-service task automation to
governed execution and faster operational change across existing systems. The two
value threads are **work faster** and **change faster**.

The owner then asked for four quantified proof points and clarified the economics:
the EUR 1.8M → EUR 300K result is **token expenditure**, not a saving on software
licenses. Tur.ai does not use one-size-fits-all models; models are matched to the
tasks being performed. The owner approved proceeding on that basis.

This is a website expression of the existing company concept, not an instruction
to silently change `tur-tc-2026/ssot/positioning.md`.

## Audience and common need

Business leaders, C-level executives, IT/transformation and process owners in
Ukraine, the Netherlands and the US. The fit is a process that crosses systems and
teams, needs documents, rules, approvals or exception handling, and cannot trade
control for speed. Industry-specific examples establish relevance without defining
a different product for each sector.

Home should let the buyer answer: What does this do? Where does it sit? What gets
better? Why believe it? What would we evaluate first?

## The story

Existing systems remain the systems of record. Tur.ai coordinates the execution
above them: AI work, system actions, human decisions, context and controls. Teams
can improve the current process and deliver the next one without making a core
replacement the prerequisite.

Explain efficiency through a concrete mechanism: task-matched models rather than
one model for everything, plus governed execution that carries context forward and
bounds retries. Do not turn this into an unverified claim about every competitor.

## Quantified proof proposed for this edition

| Theme | Display | Meaning | Source scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| Work faster | 62% | Shorter process cycles: 16 → 6 days | Customer onboarding at one European bank |
| Work faster | 82% | Lower staffing needs: 34 → 6 FTE | Same onboarding case |
| Change / scale efficiently | 83% | Lower LLM costs: EUR 1.8M → EUR 300K annual token spend | Separate banking engagement, owner-corrected cost category |
| Change faster | 3x | Faster process delivery | Source-reported European-bank transformation case |

The first three percentages are conservatively rounded down. These are selected
case results, not customer averages, guarantees or a vendor head-to-head benchmark.
The supporting 2–3 → 12+ processes/year is delivery capacity, not the arithmetic
basis of the 3x speed claim.

The owner's initial metric categories included implementation time-to-value and
scalable TCO. This edition deliberately uses **measured process time** and **token
spend** instead: it must not silently relabel them as implementation TTV or full TCO.
True implementation milestones and like-for-like portfolio TCO remain a later
evidence exercise, not a blocker to this accurately scoped copy.

## Conversion and service approach

Keep the existing **Book a Demo** and **Contact Us** actions. Frame the demo around
a relevant process, its controls and the business case. Proposed service sequence:
**PILOT → LAUNCH → SCALE**, with a baseline, acceptance criteria, and an evidence-led
decision before expansion. This is proposed service wording, not a guaranteed SLA.

## Constraints

English Home only. Keep the existing number of blocks, cards, steps, FAQ pairs,
buttons, form fields and options. Keep classes, layout, links, components, IDs,
events and form behavior. Use all 228 existing IDs and individual/segment budgets.
No Webflow changes, new vertical pages, auto-deployment or publication in this pass.

Protected quotations and hidden test/UA fields stay on hold. Existing client logos
and links are not reassigned to anonymous banking results.

## Approval boundary

The owner approved the positioning direction, the token-cost correction and the
instruction to continue. This does **not** approve every newly drafted sentence,
the proposed founder statement, changing customer attribution, or publishing the
site. Review the complete copy and the open asset issues before implementation.
